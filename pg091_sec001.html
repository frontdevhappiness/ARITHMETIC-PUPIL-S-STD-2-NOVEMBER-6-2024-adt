Exercise 8

Answer the following questions:

1.

A class has 34 pupils. How many pupils are there in 2 similar classes?

2.

A school received iron sheets from 8 businessmen.

3.

Every businessman gave 5 iron sheets. How many iron sheets did the school get?

4.

An automotive industry manufactures 12 cars every month. Find the number of cars manufactured in three months?

5.

A carpenter makes 3 tables every day. How many tables does he make in 30 days?

6.

The traffic department trains 44 drivers on road safety measures every month. How many drivers are trained in 2 months?

7.

A teacher bought 2 boxes of chalks. Each box contained 30 pieces of chalk. How many pieces of chalk did the teacher buy?

8.

A teacher taught a lesson on HIV/AIDS to standards I and II pupils. If each class had 30 pupils, how many pupils were taught the lesson on HIV/AIDS?

9.

A farmer has 2 dairy cows. Each cow produces 5 litres of milk a day. How many litres of milk does the farmer get in a day?

10.

A teacher gave an equal number of biscuits to 6 pupils. Each pupil got 4 biscuits. How many biscuits did the teacher give?

11.

John planted 4 rows of orange seedlings in his garden. He planted 12 seedlings in each row. How many seedlings did he plant?
