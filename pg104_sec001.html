Chapter Nine Recognizing fractions

Recognizing one-half of a whole object

Two equal parts of the same object form one whole object. A half is one of the two equal parts. For example, an orange can be cut into two equal pieces. Each piece is one half of the orange. Formally, one-half is also known as, a half.
