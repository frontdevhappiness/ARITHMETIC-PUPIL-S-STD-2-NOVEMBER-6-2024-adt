Adding numbers horizontally by regrouping

Numbers can be added horizontally by regrouping.

Regrouping is done by changing groups from lower place values to higher place values.

Example

167 + 138 =

Solution

The diagram shows 167 plus 138 equals 305. The green arrow connects the hundreds, the blue arrow connects the tens, and the red arrow connects the ones.

Steps

1. Add ones: 7 + 8 = 15.

Regroup 15 ones into 1 ten and 5 ones.

Write 5 in the ones place.

Take 1 ten to the tens place.

The red arrow connects 7 ones and 8 ones. The answer has 5 ones, with 1 ten carried to the tens place.

2. Add tens: 1 + 6 + 3 = 10.

Regroup 10 tens into 1 hundred and 0 tens.

Write 0 in the tens place.

Take 1 hundred to the hundreds place.

The blue arrow connects 6 tens and 3 tens. The answer has 0 tens and 5 ones, with 1 hundred carried.

3. Add hundreds: 1 + 1 + 1 = 3.

Write 3 in the hundreds place.

The green arrow connects 1 hundred and 1 hundred. With the carried hundred, the completed answer is 305.

Therefore, the answer is 305.

Or

Therefore, 167 + 138 = 305.
