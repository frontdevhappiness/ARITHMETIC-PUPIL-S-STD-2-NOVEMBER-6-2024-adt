## Status key

- `AUDIT IN PROGRESS` — current batch
- `NOT CHECKED` — awaiting its batch
- `PREVIOUSLY REPAIRED — RECHECK PENDING` — repaired before the ordered audit and will be verified again in sequence
- `COMPLETE` — all quality gates passed
