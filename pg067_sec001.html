Subtracting numbers vertically without regrouping

Numbers are subtracted vertically from the right to the left.

Example
668 −346

Solution

668 −346 322

Steps

1.
Subtract ones: 8 − 6 = 2. Write 2 in the ones place.
668 −346 2
2.
Subtract tens: 6 − 4 = 2. Write 2 in the tens place.
668 −346 22
3.
Subtract hundreds: 6 − 3 = 3. Write 3 in the hundreds place.
668 −346 322

Or

hundreds tens ones

Therefore, the answer is 322.
