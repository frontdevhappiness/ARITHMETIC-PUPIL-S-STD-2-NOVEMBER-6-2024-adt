Exercise 5

Read the following paragraph and then answer the questions that follow.

A teacher had several gifts for 6 pupils.The gifts were: 18 pencils, 12 rubbers, 30 apples and some biscuits.
1.The teacher distributed the pencils to the pupils equally.How many pencils did each pupil get?
2.The teacher distributed 60 biscuits equally.How many biscuits did each pupil get?
3.The teacher distributed the apples to the pupils equally.How many apples did each pupil get?
4.The teacher distributed the rubbers to the pupils equally.How many rubbers did each pupil get?
5.The number of pupils was increased to 9.How many pencils did each pupil get?
