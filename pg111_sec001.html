The orange is divided into three equal parts.

One piece of the orange is removed.

The piece of the orange removed is one-third.

One-third, 2/3.

The remaining two pieces of the orange are two-thirds.

Two-thirds, 2/3.

Activity

Let us play a one-third and two-thirds game. We are three pupils. We are playing a one-third and two-thirds game. We stand in a straight line. One of us in the middle holds hands with the rest. Now, we are one whole object as the picture shows.
