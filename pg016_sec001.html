Chapter Two. Arranging numbers sequentially.

Arranging numbers sequentially from the smallest to the largest

Numbers can be arranged from the smallest to the largest as follows:

Example

Non-arranged numbers: 107, 103, 109, 106, 101

Arranged numbers: 101 103 106 107 109
