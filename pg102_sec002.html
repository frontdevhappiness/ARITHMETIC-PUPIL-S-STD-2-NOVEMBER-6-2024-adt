Dividing numbers by using ICT

Numbers can be divided by using ICT.

Example
7 ÷ 1 =

Solution

Use the computer application in the TIE online library https://tie.go.tz/pages/download-software to divide numbers.

Steps

  1. 1. Open the application for division of numbers.
  2. 2. Read the question that appears on the screen clearly.
  3. 3. Type the answer by clicking on the keyboard.
  4. 4. Click OK to check your answer.
  5. 5. Do other questions to practice division of numbers.
