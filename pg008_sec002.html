Activity

Let us play a counting game

Let us stand in the form of a circle. Give each other enough distance apart to throw a ball. The first person to throw a ball should say 101. The person who catches the ball should say 102 when throwing it. The game continues until everyone is involved. This game is played by ten pupils standing and arranged in a circle.
