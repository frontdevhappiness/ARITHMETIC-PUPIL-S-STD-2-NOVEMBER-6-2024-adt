Example

Asha and Anna played a home-to-school game. Everyone played twice. Their scores were recorded as shown in the following table.

Column headings: Game number, Asha and Anna. Game 1: Asha scored 203 and Anna scored 205. Game 2: Asha scored 206 and Anna scored 211.
Game Number Asha Anna
1 203 205
2 206 211

The winner is the player who is closer to the school. Study the table and add the scores of each player.

Solution

Column headings: Game number, Asha and Anna. Game 1: Asha scored 203 and Anna scored 205. Game 2: Add 206 for Asha and add 211 for Anna. Totals: Asha scored 409 and Anna scored 416.
Game Number Asha Anna
1 203 205
2 + 206 + 211
Total 409 416

Asha's total score is 409, and Anna's total score is 416.
