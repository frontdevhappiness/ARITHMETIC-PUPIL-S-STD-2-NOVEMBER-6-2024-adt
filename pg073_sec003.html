Subtracting numbers vertically by regrouping

Numbers can be subtracted vertically by regrouping.

Example
314 314 −208 −208
hundreds tens ones
Three hundred and fourteen minus two hundred and eight equals one hundred and six.
314 −208
106
Solution
Steps
1.
Subtract the ones: 4 − 8. You cannot subtract 8 from 4, so regroup.
Three hundred and fourteen minus two hundred and eight.
314 −208
2.
Take the digit 1 from the tens place. Regroup it as 10 ones. Remember that 0 tens remain. Add the ones: 10 + 4 = 14.
Three hundred and fourteen minus two hundred and eight. The digit one in the tens place and four in the ones place are crossed out. They become zero tens and fourteen ones.
014
3 1 4 −208
3.
Subtract ones: 14 − 8 = 6. Write 6 in the ones place.
Three hundred and fourteen minus two hundred and eight. After regrouping, the ones answer is six.
014
3 1 4 −208
6
4.
Subtract tens: 0 − 0 = 0. Write 0 in the tens place.
Three hundred and fourteen minus two hundred and eight. The tens answer is zero and the ones answer is six.
014
3 1 4 −208
06
5.
Subtract hundreds: 3 − 2 = 1. Write 1 in the hundreds place.
Three hundred and fourteen minus two hundred and eight equals one hundred and six.

Therefore, the answer is 106.
