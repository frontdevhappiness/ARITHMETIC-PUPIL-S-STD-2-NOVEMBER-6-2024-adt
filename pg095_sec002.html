Dividing objects by using numbers

Example 1

Divide equally 4 oranges to 2 children. How many oranges will each child receive?

Solution

Dividing 4 oranges to 2 children can be written as,

4 ÷ 2 =

One group
divide by 2 =
Two groups

Thus, 4 ÷ 2 = 2.

Therefore, each child will get 2 oranges.
