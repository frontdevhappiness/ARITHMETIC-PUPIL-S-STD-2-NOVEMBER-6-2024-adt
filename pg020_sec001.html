Counting objects

Counting objects in ones, tens, and hundreds

Objects can be counted in ones, tens, and hundreds.

Example 1

Count the following pencils. Write their total in words.

One hundred pencils, four separate groups of ten pencils, and two individual pencils make one hundred and forty-two.
Hundreds Tens Ones
Four groups of ten pencils
Two individual pencils
One hundred Four tens Two ones
One hundred and forty-two.
