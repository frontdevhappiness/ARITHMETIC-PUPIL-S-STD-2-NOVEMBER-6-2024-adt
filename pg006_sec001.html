Introduction

This Arithmetic book has been prepared based on the 2023 Curriculum and Syllabus for Primary School Education issued by the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology. This book is the Second Edition of the Arithmetic book published in 2019 based on the 2016 curriculum. This book will enable the pupil to build arithmetic skills through various learning activities with emphasis in the following areas:

  • Mastering the basics of arithmetic
  • Mastering the skills of arithmetic
  • Applying relevant arithmetic principles
