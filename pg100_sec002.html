Arithmetic Pupil’s Book Standard Two

Exercise 4

Answer the following questions:

1.
Fourteen guavas were divided into 7 equal groups. How many guavas will be in each group?
2.
Share equally 20 buns to 5 friends. How many buns will each friend get?
3.
A bunch of 30 carrots were equally distributed in 10 small bunches. How many carrots does each bunch have?
4.
A total of 42 exercise books were equally shared to 7 pupils. How many exercise books did each pupil get?
5.
A total of 32 loaves of bread were equally distributed to 8 people. How many loaves of bread did each person get?
6.
A total of 45 pencils were equally distributed to 5 pupils. How many pencils did each pupil get?
7.
A tourist gave a gift of 64 rubbers to 8 pupils. If they equally shared them, how many rubbers did each pupil get?
8.
A total of 72 eggs were equally laid by 6 chickens. How many eggs did each chicken lay?
9.
A total of 40 books were equally distributed to 4 pupils. How many books did each pupil get?
10.
One hundred hectares of land were distributed to 10 villagers. How many hectares did each villager get?
