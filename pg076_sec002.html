Word problems on subtraction

Word problems involving subtraction are questions written in one or more sentence.

Example 1

Kabula had 956 sweets. She gave her sibling 813 sweets. How many sweets did she remain with?

Solution

Number of sweets = 956
Number of sweets given away = 813
Subtract: 956 − 813 = 143.

Steps

1.
Subtract ones: 6 − 3 = 3. Write 3 in the ones place. 956 − 813 = __3.
2.
Subtract tens: 5 − 1 = 4. Write 4 in the tens place. 956 − 813 = _43.
3.
Subtract hundreds: 9 − 8 = 1. Write 1 in the hundreds place. 956 − 813 = 143.

Therefore, Kabula remained with 143 sweets.
