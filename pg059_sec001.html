Example 2

A grandfather has 389 mango trees. A grandmother has 433 mango trees. How many mango trees do they both have?

Solution

Grandfather’s mango trees = 389 Grandmother’s mango trees = 433
Grandfather’s mango trees = 389 Grandmother’s mango trees = 433

Add the numbers vertically.

389 plus 433 equals 822

Steps

1. Add ones: 9 + 3 = 12.

Regroup 12 ones into 1 ten and 2 ones.

Write 2 in the ones place.

Take 1 ten to the tens place.

389 plus 433. Carry 1 ten. The ones digit is 2.

2. Add tens: 1 + 8 + 3 = 12.

Regroup 12 tens into 1 hundred and 2 tens.

Write 2 in the tens place.

Take 1 hundred to the hundreds place.

389 plus 433. Carry one to the hundreds place and one to the tens place. The result is twenty-two.

3. Add hundreds: 1 + 3 + 4 = 8.

Write 8 in the hundreds place.

Carry one to the hundreds place and one to the tens place. 389 plus 433 equals 822.

Therefore, they have 822 mango trees.
