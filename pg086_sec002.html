Multiplying numbers vertically

A two digit number can be multiplied by a one digit number vertically.

Example 1
1 3
× 2

Steps

1.

Multiply 3 ones by 2: 3 × 2 = 6. Write 6 in the ones place.

2.

Multiply 1 tens by 2: 1 × 2 = 2. Write 2 in the tens place.

Therefore, the answer is 26.

Example 2
3 4
× 2

Steps

1.

Multiply 4 ones by 2: 4 × 2 = 8. Write 8 in the ones place.

2.

Multiply 3 tens by 2: 3 × 2 = 6. Write 6 in the tens place.

Therefore, the answer is 68.
