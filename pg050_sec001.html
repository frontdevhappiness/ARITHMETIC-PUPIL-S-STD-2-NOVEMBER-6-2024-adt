Adding numbers vertically by regrouping

Numbers can be added vertically by regrouping.

Regrouping vertically is done in the same way as in horizontal addition.

Example 1
166 + 126

Solution

+ 1 2 6

Steps

1. Add ones: 6 + 6 = 12.

Regroup 12 ones into 1 ten and 2 ones.

Write 2 in the ones place.

Take 1 ten to the tens place.

The ones digit of the answer is 2, with 1 ten carried.
1
1 6 6
+ 1 2 6
2

2. Add tens: 1 + 6 + 2 = 9.

Write 9 in the tens place.

The tens and ones digits of the answer are 9 and 2.
1
1 6 6
+ 1 2 6
9 2

3. Add hundreds: 1 + 1 = 2.

Write 2 in the hundreds place.

The completed sum is 166 plus 126 equals 292.
